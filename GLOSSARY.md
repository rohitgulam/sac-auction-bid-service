# Auction Bid Service

Terms for the fictional dealer auction demonstration. Monetary amounts are denominated in AED.

## Language

**Demo dealer**:
A fictional dealer identity that a visitor selects by bidder code for an independent bidding session.

**Bidder code**:
A stable public code that identifies a fictional demo dealer in bids without displaying a dealer name.

**Listing**:
A vehicle or number plate offered for bidding in a timed auction.

**Live Offer**:
A listing open for bidding for 12 minutes.

**Deal Offer**:
A listing open for bidding for 72 hours.

**Closed listing**:
A listing whose bidding window has ended; its bids remain part of the auction history.

**Deposit**:
The dealer's refundable amount that backs its bidding capacity.

**Demo top-up**:
A simulated AED 10,000 or AED 50,000 addition to a demo dealer's deposit, made without transferring money and visible in wallet activity.

**Active reservation**:
The portion of a dealer's deposit committed to a leading bid or an unsettled auction win.

**Available limit**:
The deposit capacity not committed to active reservations, shown in the dealer's wallet.

**Replacement bid**:
A higher bid by the dealer already leading the same listing; it replaces that dealer's existing reservation for the listing.
